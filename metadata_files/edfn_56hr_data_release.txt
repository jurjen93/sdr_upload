<p>
In this data release, we present deep sub-arcsecond resolution images and source catalogues at 120–168 MHz covering a 2.5×2.5 square degree field of view centred on the Euclid Deep-Field North (EDFN) at 0.3″, 0.6″, and 1.2″.
The data has been produced using 56 hours of LOFAR-HBA observations, including all international stations, obtained from 7 epochs. The data were processed using the automated calibration pipelines
LINC, the DDF-pipeline, and PILOT, followed by imaging with WSClean to produce primary beam-corrected Stokes I images.
We also release a multi-resolution source catalogue containing 37791 radio sources. This catalogue combines detections in the facet images across the different resolutions.
</p>
<p>
For scientific analysis, we recommend using the individual facet images rather than the full mosaic. Each facet covers a smaller region of the field and has its own angular resolution.
</p>
